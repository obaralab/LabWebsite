Born and raised in San Diego, I developed a deep curiosity for science at an early age. Growing up in such a beautiful city with a rich scientific community, I was constantly inspired to explore the world around me. My curiosity about how things work, especially within the human body, fueled my passion for research. I’ve always believed that deepening our understanding of biochemical processes can lead to groundbreaking medical innovations. <br>
I am specifically curious about the bioorganic processes within the body that could help us better understand pharmacokinetics and efficient drug design.  My current research focuses on fluorescence microscopy in vast protein networks, where I explore the intersection of chemistry and biology in unexpected ways. <br>
Outside the lab, I enjoy hiking, going to concerts, watching movies, playing soccer, and gaming. Though I tend to be a homebody, I always cherish time spent with my closest friends and family, who continue to inspire and support me.
